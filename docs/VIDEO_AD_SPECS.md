# Video Ad Specifications

**Realty News Now: RealtyLine (Austin) and Newsline (San Antonio)**
Effective October 29, 2026. Questions or file delivery: hello@myrealtyline.com

Video ads run on the Realty News Now website and app. They play automatically with the sound off, play only while on screen, and link to your landing page when tapped.

---

## File Requirements (All Video Placements)

| Spec | Requirement |
|---|---|
| File type | MP4 (preferred) or MOV |
| Video codec | H.264 |
| Audio | AAC, optional. Videos play muted, so the message must work without sound. |
| Frame rate | 24 to 30 fps |
| Length | 6 to 30 seconds recommended; 60 seconds maximum |
| File size | Under 25 MB recommended; 100 MB maximum |
| Bitrate (guide) | About 8 to 10 Mbps for 1080p |
| Minimum resolution | 1080 pixels on the short side (1200 x 200 for the Builder Strip) |
| Thumbnail | Taken automatically from the 1-second mark. Make that frame a clear, branded shot. |

## Creative Guidelines

1. **Brand in the first 2 seconds.** Show your logo or name right away, because many viewers scroll past within a few seconds.
2. **Design for sound off.** Burn in captions or on-screen text for every spoken line.
3. **Keep text large and inside the frame.** Leave a 5% margin on all sides, and keep headlines to about 7 words.
4. **Show one clear call to action.** Examples: "Tour Today," "See Move-In Ready Homes," or "Call Now." End on it for the last 2 to 3 seconds.
5. **Make it loop.** When yours is the only ad in a placement, it repeats. A clean end frame that matches the opening looks best.
6. **No rapid flashing.** Don't flash more than 3 times per second (accessibility standard).
7. **Use real footage of the actual home or community.** Label renderings "Artist rendering."

## Required Real Estate Disclosures

- **Texas license holders (agents, teams, brokers):** Show the name of the license holder or team placing the ad. Also show the broker's name at least half the size of the largest agent or team contact information. This applies to video and social ads ([TREC advertising FAQ](https://www.trec.texas.gov/what-required-information-must-be-provided-advertisements-such-signs-email-and-business-cards)).
- **Fair Housing:** Show the Equal Housing Opportunity logo or statement on the end card. Describe the property, not the type of buyer you want.
- **Builders:** Mark pricing, incentives, and rates as "starting at" or "as of [date]," and include the required lender disclaimers.

## Running the Same Video on Social and Search (Optional)

If Realty News Now is also running your video on Meta, YouTube, or the App Store, deliver these additional versions from the same edit:

| Version | Size | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Vertical 9:16 | 1080 x 1920 (1440 x 2560 preferred for Meta) | Instagram/Facebook Reels and Stories, YouTube Shorts |
| Vertical 4:5 | 1440 x 1800 | Facebook/Instagram Feed |
| Horizontal 16:9 | 1920 x 1080 | YouTube in-stream, Google campaigns |
| Square 1:1 | 1080 x 1080 | Feed, Google campaigns |

- **Meta Reels:** Keep the top 14%, bottom 35%, and 6% on each side free of text and logos. Files can be up to 4 GB ([Meta Ads Guide](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads-guide/update/video/instagram-reels)).
- **YouTube Shorts:** Use vertical video under 60 seconds; 10 to 30 seconds works best for action-focused ads. The video must be uploaded to YouTube as public or unlisted ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/16041697?hl=en)).
- **App Store:** App preview videos run 15 to 30 seconds and up to 500 MB, at 30 fps maximum (portrait 886 x 1920) ([Apple App Store Connect](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/app-preview-specifications)).
- **Housing targeting rules:** Google housing ads can't target by age, gender, parental status, marital status, or ZIP code; radius and city targeting are allowed ([Google Ads policy](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/16701755)).
